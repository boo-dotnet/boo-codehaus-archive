### Sorin Ionescu, 03/Nov/05 9:26 AM

Yes, it could be a reference expression, but whoever does that is really a horrible coder.

About your example, of course it is failing; it does not work that way. It's designed to work with BeginFoo()/EndFoo().

Each time you call BeginFoo(), you must always call EndFoo(), regardless of whether or not you want the value returned by EndFoo(). You must call EndFoo() even it is a void.

So, it works like "fireAndForget stream.BeginRead(buffer, ...)". It will setup a closure that will be called when BeginRead() is done. That closure calls EndRead().
