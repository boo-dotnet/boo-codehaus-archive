## Description

I enforced grouping numbers by 3 if you use underscores (1_000_000), but there are cases where people group in other ways, such as groups of 4 for credit card numbers, or groups of 5 for very very large numbers. Ada and ruby allow underscores but do not enforce a particular grouping. Removing this constraint is more important when/if BOO-1 is incorporated and people use other numeric literal types like BigInteger.
