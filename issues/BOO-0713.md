but this works self.dataGridViewPhones.Columns.AddRange(*(self.PhonesColumnName, self.PhonesColumnNumber))

### Doug H, 06/Apr/06 9:18 AM

.NET's codedom doesn't distinguish arrays passed to methods accepting a variable number of parameters, because C# doesn't make such a distinction.

So SharpDevelop would have to mark a special flag telling boo codedom to explode the array, and then boo codedom will prepend an asterix to the expression:

arrayexpression.UserData["Explode"] = true

Until then, you can just manually add the asterix yourself.
