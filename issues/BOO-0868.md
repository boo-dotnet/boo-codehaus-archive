## Description

a = iterator[begin:end:step]

 would expand to 

 a = every(take(skip(iterator, begin), end-begin), step)

 where every, take and skip are functions with the following signature:

 (e as IEnumerable of T, count as int) as IEnumerable of T

 implementing the appropriate semantics.

Any component of the slice can be omitted and [::] is the identity function.
