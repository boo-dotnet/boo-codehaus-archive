## Description

Boo doesn't support numeric promotions to UIntPtr. This is needed most importantly for COM Interop issues, where EventHandlers have a signature that is (object, UIntPtr) instead of (object, IntPtr).

Bug discovery credit goes to this fellow.

http://groups.google.com/group/boolang/browse_frm/thread/68ffe781fb7e8c92/5ade61301671de6a#5ade61301671de6a
